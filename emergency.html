🚨 Right now

  1. Make sure everyone is safe. Move people away from danger.
  2. Call 911 for any medical emergency, fire, or crime in progress.
  3. Tell your Shift Supervisor right away.
  4. Don't move someone who is badly hurt unless they're in danger where they are.

Poisoning or a chemical splash? Call Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

📝 After everyone is safe

  1. Fill out an Incident Report Form before the end of your shift.
  2. Email quakertownfoodpantry@gmail.com to let the board know.
  3. The board will contact the Borough if the building or grounds were involved.

An incident is any injury, accident, argument or fight, property damage, theft, or safety hazard. Keep client details private in the report.
