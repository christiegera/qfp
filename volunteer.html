Become a Volunteer

How to join the team

1
ApplyOnline or on paper. It takes about 5 minutes.
2
Attend orientationA short walk-through of the pantry and how we work.
3
Pick your shiftsWeekly or occasional. Our coordinator works with your schedule.
Apply Online Download PDF Application

✔️ Requirements

  • Everyone attends orientation
  • 16+ can volunteer on their own; 15 and under with a parent
  • 21+ for the front desk or office
  • Youth groups need at least 2 adult supervisors

🚫 Good to know

  • We don't accept court-ordered community service
  • Youth Aid Panel referrals are welcome (email us first)
  • Current clients can't volunteer. If a volunteer needs food, please talk to the board.

Group volunteering

Churches, scouts, school clubs, and company teams are welcome, like Rotary Club, Moms & Muscles, Grace Bible Fellowship, Triple C Nursery, and East Swamp Life Group.

Email quakertownfoodpantry@gmail.com to set up your group.
